# Booking and paying

## Step 1 — Pick a package

Whetstone sells fixed packages, not hourly time. Each one has a written scope —
a plain sentence saying exactly what it covers.

Typical packages:

| Package | What it is | Example price |
|---|---|---|
| **Single session** | One focused 60-minute session on a single defined problem | A$120 |
| **Three-session engagement** | Three sessions over four weeks — diagnose, plan, then review | A$300 |

Prices vary and are shown in your currency. Whetstone supports Australian,
US, Canadian, New Zealand and Singapore dollars, pounds, euros, rupees, yen and
rand — you'll always see the symbol next to the number.

**Which one?** If you have one specific question, take a single session. If your
problem needs someone to look, think, and then check whether the plan worked,
take the three-session engagement.

### Why the scope is fixed

The scope description you see is **copied onto your booking at the moment you
book**. If Whetstone later changes what that package includes, your booking is
unaffected. What you agreed to is what you get — this is the single most
important protection on the platform, for both sides.

It also means the engagement has a defined end. An advisor isn't drifting into
being your unpaid ongoing consultant, and you aren't signing up to an open
commitment.

## Step 2 — Pick a time

Below the packages you'll see the times your advisor has made available. Pick
one and it's reserved for you.

All times are shown in one consistent time zone across the whole site, and it's
labelled on screen. The time you see is the time your advisor sees.

**If nothing suits**, book the package anyway and message your advisor to agree a
time — bookings and times can be sorted out separately. For a three-session
package, only the first session is scheduled at booking; you and your advisor
arrange the rest between you.

## Step 3 — Checkout

The checkout screen shows you, one last time:

- Who you're booking
- The package, its scope, and its session count
- The date and time
- The total, in your currency
- The insurance coverage statement

### What "payment is held" means

This is the part people ask about most, so plainly:

**Your money does not go to the advisor when you pay.** It goes to Whetstone and
is held there. The advisor is paid only after the work is done *and you've
confirmed it*. If something goes wrong before then, the money is still sitting
where it can be refunded.

```
You pay  ──►  HELD by Whetstone  ──►  released to advisor after you confirm
                     │
                     └──►  refunded to you if the engagement doesn't go ahead
```

### How you pay

Payment is taken on a secure checkout page run by Stripe, the payment processor.
Your card details go to Stripe, not to Whetstone — Whetstone never sees or stores
your card number.

You can save a card to your account for next time. See
[Account settings](11-account-settings-and-privacy.md).

### If you close the page or your card is declined

Nothing is lost. Your booking is still there, marked **Awaiting payment**, on
your **Bookings** page. Open it and try again.

## Step 4 — Confirmed

Once payment succeeds you'll see a confirmation screen and get a confirmation
email. Your advisor gets one too.

From here on your booking lives on your **Bookings** page, with a status that
tells you whose turn it is:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Awaiting payment** | You haven't paid yet — nothing is booked until you do |
| **Confirmed** | Paid, session scheduled, nothing needed from you yet |
| **In progress** | Your first session's start time has passed |
| **Confirm completion** | Your advisor says the work is done — **your turn** |
| **Completed** | Finished and the advisor has been paid |
| **Cancelled** | Called off; any payment has been refunded |
| **Disputed** | A problem was reported; the Whetstone team is on it |

## What it costs you

The package price, and nothing more. Whetstone's commission comes out of the
advisor's side, not on top of yours. There's no booking fee, no membership, and
no charge for posting a challenge or getting a shortlist.

## Cancelling

Message your advisor first — most things are sorted out in a sentence. If you
need to cancel a paid booking, use **Report a problem** on the booking, explain
the situation, and the Whetstone team will handle the refund. See
[Trust, safety and insurance](12-trust-safety-and-insurance.md).
