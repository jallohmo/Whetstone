# Choosing an advisor

## Your shortlist

When the Whetstone team has matched your challenge, you'll get an email and the
page will show **"People who can help"** — a short, curated shortlist, usually two
or three people.

It's deliberately short. This isn't a search results page you have to work
through; it's a small set of people a human has already decided are right for
your situation.

## Reading a shortlist card

Each person on the shortlist shows:

**Their name and photo** — real people, not stock avatars.

**A one-line summary** — for example, *"Retired CFO — 25 years on cash flow and
lending."*

**A "Verified" badge** — this is not decorative. It means a Whetstone team member
has personally reviewed this advisor's background, credentials and identity, and
recorded a written decision. Advisors who haven't cleared that review cannot
appear here at all.

**Why they were matched** — a short explanation of the connection between their
experience and your specific challenge, written by the person who matched you.

**Their industries and specialties** — the areas they advise on.

**Their rating** — an average from previous clients, and how many sessions it's
based on. A new advisor may have no rating yet; that's not a warning sign, it
just means they're new to the platform.

## Looking closer

Click through to an advisor's full profile to see their longer biography, their
years of experience, their complete list of specialties, and reviews written by
previous clients.

## How to choose

The team has already done the "are they qualified" part. What's left for you is
the "are they right for me" part:

- **Whose background maps onto your actual situation?** Someone who has run a
  business your size, in your circumstances, beats someone with a grander title.
- **Read a couple of their reviews.** Look for the kind of problem, not just the
  star count.
- **Trust your read of their summary and bio.** You're going to spend an hour
  talking candidly with this person.

There's no penalty for taking a day to decide, and no auto-expiry on your
shortlist.

## What if nobody feels right?

Reply to your shortlist email and say so — including *why*, if you can put your
finger on it. Getting the second shortlist right is much easier when the team
knows what missed the first time. You are never charged for a shortlist.

## Booking without a shortlist

If you already know who you want — someone you were matched with before, or an
advisor whose profile you were sent — you can go straight to their profile and
book from there. You don't have to post a challenge first.
