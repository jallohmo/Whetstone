# Glossary

Every word you'll meet on Whetstone, in plain English.

**Advisor** — A semi-retired or retired professional who gives paid advisory
sessions. Must pass human verification before appearing anywhere on the platform.

**Auto-accept** — If a client doesn't respond seven days after an advisor marks
work complete, the booking confirms itself and the advisor is paid. Prevents
advisors being left unpaid by silence.

**Availability slot** — A specific date and time an advisor has opened for
booking. Clients book directly into one.

**Awaiting confirmation** — The advisor has said the work is done and the client
hasn't confirmed yet. The money is still held.

**Awaiting payment** — A booking created but not paid for. Nothing is reserved
until payment goes through.

**Booking** — One engagement between a client and an advisor: a package, a price,
a scope and one or more sessions.

**Bounded scope** — The written description of exactly what a package covers,
copied onto your booking at the moment you book so it can never change
afterwards.

**Challenge** (also **need**) — The business problem a client describes when
asking for help. Private to the client and the Whetstone team.

**Client** — The business owner buying advice. Called "customer" in some places
on the site.

**Commission** — Whetstone's cut, taken from the advisor's side of the package
price. Clients pay the listed price and nothing more.

**Confirmed** — Paid for and scheduled.

**Currency** — Whetstone works in several: Australian, US, Canadian, New Zealand
and Singapore dollars, pounds sterling, euros, Indian rupees, Japanese yen and
South African rand. Prices always show their symbol.

**Disputed** — A problem has been reported on a booking. Payment is frozen and a
Whetstone team member is handling it.

**Held** — The state your payment sits in between paying and the work being
confirmed complete. It's with Whetstone, not the advisor, and can still be
refunded.

**In progress** — The first session's start time has passed and the engagement is
under way.

**Insurance coverage** — Whetstone's professional indemnity policy covering advice
given in sessions booked on the platform. Automatic; if it weren't active,
payments would be refused.

**Match / matching** — The process where a Whetstone team member reads a client's
challenge and hand-picks suitable advisors. Done by people, not an algorithm.

**No show** — A session where one party didn't attend. Advisors record this
against the session; it's the evidence in any later dispute.

**Ops** — The Whetstone operations team: the people who verify advisors, match
challenges and resolve disputes.

**Outcome survey** — Short private questions after a booking (did it help, would
you rebook). Not published; used to improve future matching.

**Package** — What you actually buy — a single session, or a multi-session
engagement — with a fixed price and a written scope.

**Payout** — Money moving from Whetstone to an advisor's bank account after
release.

**Recovery codes** — One-time codes issued when you turn on two-factor
authentication, for signing in if you lose your phone. Using one turns two-factor
off so you can set it up again.

**Release** — The moment held money is sent on to the advisor, triggered by the
client confirming completion (or by auto-accept).

**Review** — A public rating out of 5 and optional comment, shown on the advisor's
profile.

**Session** — One scheduled meeting, normally 60 minutes over video. A package
contains one or more.

**Shortlist** — The small set of advisors the Whetstone team selects for a
client's challenge, with a written reason for each.

**Specialty** — An area an advisor advises on, chosen from the industry list.
Used by the team when matching.

**Stripe** — The payment company Whetstone uses. Holds card and bank details so
Whetstone doesn't have to.

**Two-factor authentication (2FA)** — An extra sign-in step using a 6-digit code
from an app on your phone.

**Verified** — The badge shown on an advisor who has passed identity, credential
and background review by a Whetstone team member. Unverified advisors are invisible
and unbookable.
