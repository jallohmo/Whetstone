# Applying and getting verified

*For semi-retired and retired professionals who want to advise.*

## What you're signing up for

Short, paid, clearly-bounded advisory sessions with business owners who have a
specific problem in your field. One hour at a time, over video, from wherever you
are. You choose your own hours, and you can go quiet for a month without
explaining yourself to anyone.

You're not being hired, contracted or placed. Each booking is its own small
engagement with a written scope, and it ends when that scope is met.

## Applying

Start at **Become an advisor**. You'll create an account, then complete the
application. Allow about twenty minutes, and have your documents to hand.

### Your background

**One-line summary.** The first thing a business owner reads. Concrete beats
impressive:

> *Retired CFO — 25 years on cash flow and lending.*
>
> *Ran a 40-person commercial kitchen for two decades.*
>
> *Former practice principal — succession and handover.*

**About you.** A few paragraphs on where you've worked and what you're genuinely
good at helping with. This is not a CV. The business owner reading it wants to
know whether you've stood where they're standing.

Worth including: the kinds of business you've worked in and their size, the
problems you've solved repeatedly, and the situations you'd rather not advise on.
Saying what you *don't* do builds trust and saves you mismatched bookings.

**Years of experience.** Total professional years in your field.

### What you can advise on

Pick your areas from the industry list — as many as genuinely apply. These are
what the Whetstone team searches when matching a business owner to advisors, so
under-selecting means fewer bookings, and over-selecting means the wrong ones.

**"Something not listed?"** — there's a free-text box. If your industry or a
capability isn't in the list, describe it. A real person reads it, and the list
gets extended when a gap is genuine. You will never be stuck because a menu
didn't have your job in it.

### Credentials and ID

Upload:

- **Proof of identity** — passport or driver's licence
- **Credentials** — professional registrations, licences, memberships,
  qualifications relevant to what you'll advise on

These go into private storage. **They are never shown to business owners** — only
the Whetstone team reviewing your application can see them. What clients see is
the *result*: a Verified badge.

## What happens next

Your application goes into a queue and a real person reviews it. Nothing is
approved or rejected automatically.

They check three things:

1. **Identity** — you are who you say you are
2. **Credentials** — the licences and registrations you claim are real and current
3. **Background** — usually a reference call about the experience you've described

Every decision is written down with a note explaining it. That record exists for
your protection as much as anyone's.

## Tracking your application

**Your verification** in the advisor menu shows exactly where you are:

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Application submitted** | Received, waiting to be picked up | Nothing |
| **Under review** | Someone is working through it | Nothing — you may get a reference call |
| **More information needed** | Something is missing or unclear | Read the note, update your application, resubmit |
| **Verified & live** | You're approved | Add availability — see the next guide |
| **Not approved** | The application wasn't accepted | The note explains why |

### If you're asked for more information

Open your application, fix or add what was asked for, and submit again. That puts
you back in the review queue — it's a normal part of the process, not a strike
against you.

## While you're pending

Until you're verified, your profile is **not visible to business owners**, doesn't
appear on any shortlist, and can't be booked. This is the platform's core promise
and there's no way around it — including for people with impeccable credentials.

## Once you're verified

- Your profile becomes visible and bookable
- You can appear on shortlists the team assembles
- **Add your availability** — nobody can book you without it
- **Set up payouts** before your first session ends, so your money can reach you

See [Your availability and
bookings](09-advisor-availability-and-bookings.md) and [Earnings and
payouts](10-advisor-earnings-and-payouts.md).

## Keeping your profile current

Edit your public profile any time from **Account**. Editing it does **not** reset
your verification — once you're verified, you stay verified. (Only a full
re-application after a "more information needed" goes back through review.)
