# Earnings and payouts

## How the money moves

```
Client pays  ──►  HELD by Whetstone  ──►  released when the client confirms
                                          (or automatically after 7 days)
                                                    │
                                                    ▼
                                          your bank account, via Stripe
```

The client's payment doesn't reach you at the moment they book. It's held. That
protects them — and it also protects you, because it proves the money exists
before you do the work. Nobody on Whetstone works for a client who hasn't paid.

## What you're paid

Whetstone takes a commission from the package price; you receive the rest. The
commission rate is stated on your earnings page and applied identically to every
advisor.

Your earnings page shows the split for every booking, so you always see the gross
and the amount coming to you.

There are no fees to join, no listing fees and no charge for holding availability.

## Setting up payouts

Do this before your first session ends, so nothing is waiting when your money is
released.

Go to **Earnings** → **Set up payouts**. That hands you over to Stripe, the
payment processor, who will ask for:

- Your legal name and address
- Your date of birth
- Your bank account details
- Sometimes an ID document, depending on your country

**Whetstone never sees your bank details.** They go to Stripe, who move the money.

If you don't finish in one go, come back to the same button and pick up where you
left off.

## When you're paid

The release is triggered by the client confirming the work is done — or
automatically seven days after you mark it complete, if they don't. You get an
email when it's released.

After release, the money moves from Stripe to your bank on your payout schedule.
Bank transfer times vary by country; a few business days is typical.

**Payout schedule.** In **Account** → **Payouts**, you can have earnings released
to you weekly, or held and paid less frequently. Weekly is the default.

## Reading your earnings page

- **Released** — paid out to you, or on its way to your bank
- **Held** — the client has paid, and the work isn't confirmed complete yet
- The chart shows earnings over recent months

## If something goes wrong

**A client reports a problem.** The booking is parked and no money moves until a
Whetstone team member has looked at it. You'll be asked for your side. Outcomes
range from resolving in your favour (you're paid) to a refund to the client.
Session outcomes you recorded are the evidence that matters here — which is why
recording them is worth the ten seconds.

**A client no-shows.** Record the session as a no-show. Don't quietly absorb it —
the record is what any later conversation rests on.

**Money hasn't arrived.** Check, in order: is the booking confirmed complete on
your bookings page; does your earnings page show it released; is your payout
setup finished; has enough time passed for your bank. If all four are yes, contact
support.

## Tax

Whetstone doesn't withhold tax. You're advising as an independent professional and
what you receive is yours to declare in your own country. Your earnings page and
Stripe's records are your source figures. Speak to your own accountant about how
this income should be treated where you live — Whetstone can't advise on that.
