# Creating your account

## Which kind of account do you need?

| You are… | Sign up here | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| A business owner looking for help | **Sign up** | Post challenges, receive shortlists, book and pay |
| A professional wanting to advise | **Become an advisor** | Apply, get verified, set your times, get paid |

You choose this once, at signup, and it shapes the whole site you see afterwards.
If you pick the wrong one, contact support rather than making a second account —
two accounts on the same email address won't work.

> **Whetstone staff accounts** are never created by signing up. They're created
> internally by the Whetstone team.

## Signing up

You have three ways in, and they all end up in the same place:

1. **Email and password.** Enter your email, choose a password, and you're done.
2. **Continue with Google.**
3. **Continue with LinkedIn.**

Google and LinkedIn are usually the fastest — there's no password to remember and
your name and photo come across automatically.

### Password rules

If you use a password:

- It must be **at least 8 characters**.
- Whetstone checks it against a public list of passwords that have leaked in past
  data breaches elsewhere on the internet. If yours is on that list, you'll be
  asked to pick a different one. **Your password itself is never sent anywhere**
  during this check — only a scrambled fragment of it is used, which can't be
  turned back into your password.

A short phrase you'll remember ("purple kettle nine tuesday") beats a short
scramble ("P@ss1!") on both counts.

### Confirming your email

If you signed up with email and password, Whetstone sends you a confirmation
link. Click it and you'll be signed in and returned to whatever you were doing.

**Can't find the email?** Check your spam or junk folder, and search for
"Whetstone". The link is single-use — if you've already clicked it once, just
sign in normally instead.

## "I clicked something and it asked me to sign up first"

That's deliberate. Describing a business challenge requires an account, because
your challenge needs an owner — that's what lets you see it later, lets the team
email you when your shortlist is ready, and keeps it private to you.

Whetstone remembers where you were going. Sign up, and you'll land straight back
on the form you were filling in.

## Signing in

Go to **Log in** and use whichever method you signed up with. If you signed up
with Google, sign in with Google — a password won't have been set on that
account.

### If you've turned on two-factor authentication

After your password, you'll be asked for a 6-digit code from your authenticator
app. See [Account settings and security](11-account-settings-and-privacy.md) for
how this works and what to do if you lose your phone.

## Forgotten password

1. On the log-in screen, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link and follow it.
4. Choose a new password — the same 8-character minimum and leaked-password check
   apply.

For your security, Whetstone gives the same "check your email" message whether or
not that address has an account. So if nothing arrives, the most likely reason is
that you signed up with a different email address, or through Google or LinkedIn.

## Signing out

Click your name or photo in the top-right corner and choose **Sign out**. Do this
on any shared or public computer.
