# Your availability and bookings

## Your dashboard

Signing in as an advisor takes you to your dashboard: your earnings so far,
upcoming sessions, recent reviews, and unread messages. Everything else is in the
menu down the left.

## Adding availability

**Nobody can book you until you add times.** This is the single most common
reason a newly verified advisor hears nothing.

Go to **Availability**:

1. Pick a date and time.
2. Pick a duration (60 minutes is standard).
3. Click **Add**.

Each slot appears in your list straight away. Nothing saves until you click Add —
there's no accidental-tap risk.

### The rules

- **Times must be in the future.** You'll be told, plainly, if you pick one
  that's passed.
- **No duplicates.** If you already have a slot at that time, you'll be told
  rather than silently getting nothing.
- **All times are in one consistent zone**, labelled on screen, and it's the same
  zone your client sees. There is no time-zone maths for either of you to do.

### Removing a slot

Click remove next to it. **A slot that's already booked can't be removed** — that
would strand a client who has paid. If you genuinely can't make a booked session,
message the client and agree a new time instead.

### How much to put up

Put up more than you expect to use. Empty slots cost you nothing; a client
looking at a single available Tuesday usually books someone else. Adding a few
weeks at a time works well.

## When someone books you

You get an email. The booking appears under **Bookings** with:

- The client and their business
- The package they bought, and **its written scope**
- The session time
- What you'll be paid

**Read the scope.** It's what the client agreed to and what you're being paid to
deliver — and it's locked to that booking, so it can't shift under you later.

## Before the session

Use the booking's message thread to get context in advance. A short note — "send
me your last three months' figures and the two questions you most want answered"
— makes an hour go much further.

You'll get reminder emails 24 hours and 1 hour ahead.

## Running the session

Open the booking and use **Join** at the scheduled time. It opens a private video
room in your browser — nothing to install.

Practical notes:
- Join two minutes early; there's a check screen for camera and microphone
- Headphones, decent light, wired connection where possible
- The room is private to you and the client

If video won't cooperate, agree a phone call in the thread. It doesn't affect the
booking or your payment.

## After each session: record the outcome

On the booking, mark how the session went:

- **Completed** — it happened
- **No show** — the client didn't attend
- **Rescheduled** — moved to another time

You can only do this once the start time has passed.

This matters more than it looks. It's the record the platform runs on: you can't
close a booking until every session has an outcome, and if there's ever a dispute
about a no-show, this is the evidence that gets looked at.

## Marking the work complete

Once every session in the package has an outcome, **Mark complete** becomes
available.

That does two things: it tells the client the work is done, and it starts the
clock on your payment.

You can't mark a three-session package complete after one session — the button
stays disabled and tells you what's outstanding.

## Then what?

The client confirms, and you're paid. If they don't:

- **After 3 days** they get a reminder
- **After 7 days** it's confirmed automatically and you're paid anyway

You are not dependent on a client remembering to come back. See [Earnings and
payouts](10-advisor-earnings-and-payouts.md).

## Reviews

After confirming, clients can leave a rating and a comment, which appear on your
profile. They also fill in a short private survey that helps the team match you
better in future.

A new advisor with no reviews is not disadvantaged on shortlists — the team
matches on experience, and writes the reason the client sees.

## Taking a break, or leaving

In **Account** → **Leave the platform** you can deactivate. That hides your
profile from matching and search and stops new bookings, but keeps your record so
you can come back whenever you like.

You can't deactivate while you have live bookings — those need honouring first.
