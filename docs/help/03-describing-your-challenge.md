# Describing your challenge

This is the first real step, and it's the one that decides how good your
shortlist is. It takes about five minutes.

Start from **Describe your business challenge** on the home page, or **Post a
challenge** once you're signed in.

## The form, field by field

### "What's your business called?"

Your trading name. Your advisor sees this so they know who they're helping. If
you don't have a formal business name, your own name is fine.

You only fill this in once — it's remembered for next time.

### "What kind of business do you run?"

Pick the closest industry from the list. Don't agonise over it: this is a
starting point for matching, not a filing decision.

### "Anything more specific?" (optional)

Once you've picked an industry, a second list appears with sub-specialties inside
it. Choosing one sharpens the match. If nothing fits, leave it alone — the
industry on its own is enough.

### "What's the challenge?"

A few words that name the problem. Think of it as the subject line.

Good examples:
- "Pricing has drifted below cost"
- "Cash flow gap every quarter"
- "Handing the business to my daughter"
- "Failing a compliance audit"

### "Tell us what's actually going on"

The important one. This is what a Whetstone team member actually reads when
choosing who to put in front of you.

**Write it like you'd explain it to a friend over coffee.** Plain language is
genuinely better than business language here. Nobody is judging your writing.

Things worth including:

- What's happening, and for how long
- What you've already tried
- What "solved" would look like for you
- Anything that constrains the answer — a deadline, a bank, a family member, a
  regulator, a lease

Things you don't need:
- Financial statements or documents (share those directly with your advisor later
  if they're relevant)
- A proposed solution — that's what you're hiring for

> **Be honest about the messy parts.** The more real the description, the better
> the match. An advisor picked for a tidied-up version of your problem will spend
> the first session discovering the real one.

## What happens after you submit

1. You're taken to a page that says **"Finding the right people"**. That's the
   normal state — it means your challenge has been received and is with the
   Whetstone team.
2. A real person reads it and hand-picks advisors whose actual career matches
   your situation. This is not an automatic keyword match.
3. When the shortlist is ready, **you get an email**, and that same page turns
   into your shortlist.

You don't need to keep the page open, and you don't need to chase anything. You
can close the browser and come back later.

## Where to find it again

Your challenges are listed on your **Home** page once you're signed in. Click one
to see its shortlist (or its "still being matched" status).

## Can I post more than one challenge?

Yes. Post a separate challenge for each distinct problem rather than combining
them — they may well need different advisors, and each gets its own shortlist.

## Who can see what I wrote?

Only you and the Whetstone team. It is never published, never shown to other
business owners, and not visible to advisors in general. An advisor sees the
context of your situation once you book them.
