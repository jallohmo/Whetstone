# Your sessions

## Where everything lives

Open **Bookings** and click the booking. That page is the home for that
engagement: the messages, the session times, the video call button, and the
buttons for finishing up.

## Messaging your advisor

Every booking has its own private message thread. Use it to:

- Send context ahead of the session — figures, a document, background
- Agree the times for sessions two and three of a package
- Ask a short follow-up afterwards
- Say you need to reschedule

**Only you and your advisor can post in it.** The Whetstone team can read a
thread if a problem is reported on the booking, but cannot write in it.

You'll get an email when your advisor replies (you can turn that off in
[notification settings](11-account-settings-and-privacy.md)).

Messages aren't instant chat — your advisor might reply in a few hours, not a
few seconds. That's normal.

## Reminders

Whetstone emails you twice before each session:

- **24 hours before**
- **1 hour before**

Each reminder goes out once. If you book something at short notice, you'll just
get the one-hour reminder — the wording adjusts to how soon it actually is.

## Joining the video call

When it's time, open the booking and use the **Join** button on the session. It
opens the video room in your browser.

**Before your first session:**

- Use Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox — kept reasonably up to date
- Allow the browser to use your camera and microphone when it asks
- There's a check screen before you enter, so you can see and hear yourself
  first
- Join a couple of minutes early
- A wired or strong Wi-Fi connection beats mobile data
- Headphones remove most echo problems

You don't need to install anything, and you don't need an account with any video
service. The room is private to the two of you and is only open around the
scheduled time.

**If the Join button doesn't work**, message your advisor — you can agree a phone
call instead. It doesn't affect the booking or the payment.

## Getting the most out of an hour

- Send your advisor the background in the message thread beforehand rather than
  spending the first fifteen minutes on it
- Have your three or four real questions written down
- Say early what you want to walk away with
- Take notes, or ask your advisor to summarise the actions in the thread
  afterwards

## Rescheduling

Message your advisor and agree a new time. For a single-session booking, they can
record the original as rescheduled and you agree a replacement. No payment
changes and nothing needs to be re-booked.

## Multi-session packages

For a three-session engagement, only the first session is scheduled when you
book. Arrange the second and third in the message thread as you go — that's
intentional, since the right gap depends on what you're working through.

The booking can't be closed until every session in the package has actually
happened. Your advisor cannot mark a three-session package complete after
session one.
