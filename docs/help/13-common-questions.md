# Common questions and problems

## Signing in

**I can't sign in and my password isn't working.**
Most often the account was created with Google or LinkedIn, in which case there's
no Whetstone password. Try those buttons. Otherwise use **Forgot password**.

**I never got the confirmation email.**
Check spam and junk, and search for "Whetstone". Confirm you used the address you
think you did. The link only works once — if you've already used it, just sign in.

**I never got the password reset email.**
Same checks. Whetstone shows the same message whether or not an address has an
account, so silence usually means a different address was used — or a social
login.

**It's asking for a 6-digit code.**
You have two-factor authentication on. Open your authenticator app for the
current code. No phone? Use a recovery code — that signs you in *and* switches
two-factor off so you can set it up again. Neither? Contact support.

**I signed up as the wrong type of account.**
Contact support. Don't create a second account on the same email — it won't work.

## Posting a challenge

**It made me sign up before letting me post.**
By design: your challenge needs an owner so you can find it later and be emailed
when the shortlist is ready. After signing up you're returned to the same form.

**My industry isn't in the list.**
Pick the closest and explain the specifics in the description — a person reads
it, and the description carries more weight than the dropdown.

**How long until I get a shortlist?**
A person is assembling it, so it's not instant. You'll be emailed when it's
ready; there's no need to keep the page open.

**Can I edit a challenge after posting?**
Not directly. Add the missing context by replying to your shortlist email, or
post a fresh challenge if it's changed substantially.

## Advisors and shortlists

**Only two people on my shortlist — is that all?**
Yes, and that's intentional. Short lists of well-chosen people beat long lists you
have to sift.

**None of them feel right.**
Say so in a reply, including why. There's no charge, and a second shortlist is
much better aimed once the team knows what missed.

**An advisor has no reviews.**
They're new to Whetstone, not unchecked. Every advisor passed the same human
verification before appearing at all.

## Booking and payment

**My card was declined / I closed the page.**
Nothing is lost. The booking sits on your **Bookings** page as *Awaiting payment*
— open it and try again.

**When is my advisor paid?**
When you confirm the work is complete, or automatically seven days after they
mark it done if you haven't responded and haven't reported a problem.

**Are there fees on top of the price?**
No. The package price is the whole cost to you. Whetstone's commission comes out
of the advisor's side.

**Can I get a refund?**
If the engagement doesn't go ahead, or something goes wrong, use **Report a
problem** on the booking. Payment is held rather than sent on, so a refund is
straightforward at that stage.

**What currency am I charged in?**
The one shown next to the price at checkout. Whetstone supports several — your
bank may apply its own conversion if it differs from your account currency.

## Sessions

**The Join button doesn't do anything.**
Try a different browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox), allow camera and
microphone access when asked, and turn off any VPN. The room only opens around
the scheduled time. If it still won't work, message your advisor and do it by
phone — the booking and payment are unaffected.

**They can't hear me.**
Check the microphone selected on the pre-join screen, unmute, and use headphones.

**I need to reschedule.**
Message the other party in the booking thread and agree a new time. Nothing needs
re-booking and no payment changes.

**Nobody turned up.**
Wait ten minutes, then message. Advisors should record the session as a no-show;
clients should use **Report a problem** if it isn't resolved.

## Finishing

**I got an email saying my advisor marked the work complete.**
Open the booking and confirm — that's what releases their payment. If it isn't
actually done, use **Report a problem** instead.

**I confirmed by accident.**
Contact support immediately. Confirming releases the payment, so the sooner the
better.

**I don't want to leave a review.**
You don't have to. It doesn't affect anything.

## For advisors

**I'm verified but nothing's happening.**
Almost always missing availability — nobody can book you without slots. Add
several weeks' worth. Also check your summary and specialties: those are what the
matching team searches.

**Mark complete is greyed out.**
Every session in the package needs a recorded outcome first. Record the ones
you've done, and for a multi-session package, do the remaining sessions.

**I can't remove a booked slot.**
Correct — a client has paid for it. Message them and agree a new time.

**My payout hasn't arrived.**
Check: is the booking confirmed complete; does the earnings page show it
released; is your Stripe payout setup finished; has your bank had a few business
days. If all four, contact support.

**Do I have to accept every booking?**
If the work is outside what you can help with, say so in the thread straight
away — better a clean refund than a wasted hour for both of you.

**Can I take a break?**
Yes. Remove your future availability, or deactivate your profile from **Account**
and reactivate when you're ready.

## Still stuck?

Contact support with your account email, the booking involved if there is one,
and what you expected to happen versus what did.
