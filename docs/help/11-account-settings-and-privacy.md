# Account settings, security and privacy

Everything here is under **Account**, reachable from your name or photo in the
top-right corner.

## Profile

**If you're a business owner**, this is your name, phone number, photo and
business name. Your advisor sees your name and business name; nobody else does.

**If you're an advisor**, this is your *public* profile — the summary, biography,
years of experience and specialties that business owners read. Edits go live
straight away, and **editing does not reset your verification**. Once verified,
you stay verified.

### Your photo

Click your avatar to upload one, or remove it. For advisors, it appears on your
public profile and on shortlist cards. A plain, friendly headshot does more for
bookings than most people expect.

## Security

### Changing your password

Enter your current password, then the new one (8 characters minimum, and it's
checked against known leaked passwords). If you signed in with Google or LinkedIn
there's no Whetstone password to change — manage it with them instead.

### Two-factor authentication (2FA)

Strongly recommended, especially for advisors receiving payouts.

**Turning it on:**

1. Choose **Enable two-factor**.
2. Scan the square barcode with an authenticator app on your phone — Google
   Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy and 1Password all work. If you
   can't scan, type in the key shown underneath.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows to confirm.

From then on, signing in asks for your password *and* a fresh code from the app.
The codes change every thirty seconds; you don't need internet on the phone for
them to work.

**Recovery codes — do not skip this.** When you enable 2FA you're shown a list of
one-time recovery codes. This is the only time you'll see them.

- Save them somewhere that isn't your phone — a password manager, or printed and
  put with your important papers
- Each one works once
- Using one to sign in **switches two-factor off**, so you can set it up again on
  your new phone

You can generate a fresh set at any time from the same screen. Doing so cancels
the old set.

**Lost your phone and your codes?** Contact support. Recovering an account with
neither is deliberately slow, because the alternative is an account anyone can
talk their way into.

## Notifications

Choose which emails you get. Everyone gets the essentials — payment receipts,
confirmations and security notices — regardless of these settings.

**Business owners:**

| Setting | What it is | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Session reminders | A nudge before each booked session | On |
| Booking updates | When an advisor marks work complete and needs your confirmation | On |
| New messages | When an advisor replies in your thread | On |
| Product updates | Occasional news about Whetstone | Off |

**Advisors:**

| Setting | What it is | Default |
|---|---|---|
| New bookings | When a client books one of your slots | On |
| New messages | When a client replies in a thread | On |
| Payout confirmations | When held earnings are released to you | On |

Leaving booking updates and session reminders on is wise — they're how you find
out something needs you.

## Payments (business owners)

Save a card so you don't retype it every time. Cards are stored by Stripe, the
payment processor — **Whetstone never sees or holds your card number**. Remove a
saved card any time; removing it doesn't affect bookings you've already paid for.

## Leaving

### Advisors: deactivating

**Account** → **Leave the platform**. Your profile is hidden from matching and
search, and no new bookings can be made. Your record is kept, and you can
reactivate whenever you like.

You can't deactivate while you have live bookings — honour those first.

### Business owners: deleting your account

**Account** → **Delete account**. This is permanent.

Two things will stop it:

- **You have live bookings.** Finish or cancel them first.
- **You have past bookings.** Accounts with booking history can't be
  self-deleted, because those bookings carry payment and audit records that must
  be retained. Contact support and they'll handle it properly.

For an account with no booking history, deletion removes your profile and signs
you out immediately.

## Your privacy in short

- **Your challenge description** is seen by you and the Whetstone team only. It's
  never published and never shown to advisors at large.
- **Your messages** are between you and the other party. The Whetstone team can
  read a thread if a problem is reported on that booking, and can never post in
  one.
- **Advisor ID and credential documents** are private to the reviewing team.
  Clients only ever see the Verified badge.
- **Card and bank details** are held by Stripe, not Whetstone.
- **Reviews you write** are public on the advisor's profile. **The outcome survey
  is not** — it's used internally to improve matching.

The full legal detail is in the Privacy Policy and Terms, linked at the bottom of
every page.
