# What Whetstone is

## The short version

Whetstone is a marketplace. On one side are **business owners** with a specific
problem. On the other are **semi-retired and retired professionals** — people who
spent decades doing the thing you're currently stuck on.

You describe your challenge. A real person on the Whetstone team reads it and
puts together a short shortlist of advisors who have actually done that work. You
pick one, book a session, and pay. The money sits safely with Whetstone until the
work is finished.

## What makes it different from business coaching

Three things:

**1. Everyone is checked.** An advisor cannot appear on your shortlist, take a
booking, or be paid until a Whetstone team member has reviewed their background,
their credentials and their ID. Nobody is approved automatically.

**2. Every session is insured.** Whetstone carries a professional indemnity
policy that covers the advice given in sessions booked through the platform. You
don't arrange anything — it's already in place, and you'll see it stated on the
booking screen before you pay.

**3. The scope is fixed in advance.** You don't buy open-ended "coaching". You
buy a package with a written description of exactly what it covers — one session
on one defined problem, or three sessions over four weeks to diagnose, plan and
review. That description is locked to your booking, so it can't change after you
pay.

## Who it's for

- **Any industry.** Whetstone launched across all industries at once, from
  construction to healthcare to hospitality to software.
- **Anywhere in the world.** Prices are shown in your currency; sessions happen
  over video.
- **Any size of business.** Most people using it are owner-operators or small
  teams without a board or an advisory network to call on.

## What it costs

You pay the package price and nothing else. There's no membership, no monthly
fee, and no charge for posting a challenge or receiving a shortlist. Whetstone
takes its commission out of what the advisor receives, not on top of what you
pay.

## What Whetstone is not

- It's not a recruitment service — advisors don't join your business.
- It's not ongoing consulting — engagements are deliberately bounded.
- It's not a directory you search alone — a human does the matching.
- It's not legal, medical or financial *representation*. Advisors give advice
  based on their professional experience; acting on it stays your decision.
