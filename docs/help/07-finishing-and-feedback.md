# Finishing up and leaving feedback

## How an engagement ends

Two steps, one from each side:

1. **Your advisor marks the work done.** They can only do this once every session
   in your package has actually taken place.
2. **You confirm it.** That's what releases their payment.

You'll get an email — *"[Advisor] has marked your session complete"* — and your
booking moves to the status **Confirm completion**.

## Confirming

Open the booking and choose **Confirm completion**. The screen shows you what was
agreed (the scope you booked) so you can check it against what actually happened.

Confirming means: *the work described here was done.* It releases the held
payment to your advisor, minus Whetstone's commission.

It doesn't commit you to a good review, and it isn't a legal waiver. It's the
"yes, that happened" step.

## If it didn't go to plan

On the same screen there's a **Report a problem** option. Use it. Explain what
went wrong in your own words.

Reporting a problem **parks the booking and stops any payment moving**. That's
the whole reason this confirmation step exists. A Whetstone team member picks it
up and works out the right outcome with both of you — that might be a further
session, a partial resolution, or a refund.

Don't confirm and then complain. Confirming releases the money, which makes
everything harder to unwind.

## The 3-day and 7-day rules

Life gets busy, so:

- **After 3 days**, if you haven't confirmed, Whetstone sends you one reminder
  email.
- **After 7 days**, if you still haven't confirmed and haven't reported a
  problem, the booking is confirmed automatically and your advisor is paid.

This exists so that advisors aren't left unpaid indefinitely by a client who
simply moved on. If something *did* go wrong, report it inside those seven days
rather than staying quiet.

## Leaving a review

After confirmation you'll be invited to leave feedback. It's optional but genuinely
useful — it's what the next business owner reads when choosing.

There are two parts:

**The public review**
- A rating out of 5
- A written comment (optional)

This appears on the advisor's profile and shortlist card.

**The outcome survey**
- *Did this address your problem?* — Yes / Partially / No
- *Would you book this advisor again?* — Yes / No
- Anything else you want to add

The survey is not published. It's how Whetstone learns which matches actually
work, and it feeds back into how future shortlists are put together.

Write the review you wish you'd been able to read: what your problem was, what
the advisor did, and what changed. That's far more useful than "great session".

## Booking the same advisor again

Go to their profile and book a new package. There's no need to post a new
challenge — though if the problem is genuinely a different one, posting it gets
you a fresh opinion on who's best placed to help.
